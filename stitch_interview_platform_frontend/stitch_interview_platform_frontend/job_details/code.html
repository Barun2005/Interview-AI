Role Overview

As a Senior Product Designer at InterviewPro, you will lead the evolution of our "Guided Confidence" design system. You'll work closely with engineering and product partners to build seamless, high-utility experiences for candidates and interviewers globally.

We're looking for someone who balances high-fidelity craft with strategic thinking, ensuring that every interaction reduces anxiety and promotes clarity.

  • Develop and scale accessible UI components.
  • Lead end-to-end design for core interview workspaces.
  • Mentor junior designers and advocate for design quality.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to define product roadmaps.
